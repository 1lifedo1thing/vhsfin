# Press sounds, cut from the clips above (also CC0):
# ui_key_*.wav    — the eject button click, 90ms from vhs_eject at 0.07s
# ui_select_*.wav — the slot flap click, 70ms from vhs_insert at 0.31s, with
#                   its springy ring (choosing a shelf, TV, sort order)
# ui_tick_*.wav   — the first 30ms of the eject button click, high-passed
#                   at 1.8 kHz with a 4.5ms decay: a dry tick without the
#                   ring (icons, close, menus, checkboxes, rows)
# ui_switch_*.wav — the cassette pop from vhs_eject at 0.97s, low-passed at
#                   ~2.2 kHz and pitched down ~12% for a heavier switch chonk

# the app rotates so repeated presses don't sound identical.
